## Design Context

### Users

Barista’s public site is for platform engineers and agent builders evaluating infrastructure for long-running, stateful workloads. They need to understand quickly whether Barista can let an agent session, development environment, or runtime stop consuming compute and later continue from the same in-memory context. They read the page as a technical product evaluation, not as a consumer coffee brand experience.

### Brand Personality

Serene, precise, editorial. Barista should feel technically credible and measured, with warmth coming from language, materials, and typography rather than playful decoration or saturated color. The voice is concise, assured, and honest about what exists today versus what remains on the roadmap.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, warm, refined editorial direction. Favor warm paper-like neutrals, dark coffee-tinted ink, hairline rules, generous negative space, and one restrained accent. Typography should carry the identity through a distinctive display face paired with a highly readable text face. Keep the coffee metaphor subtle in naming and small service details; avoid oversized cup illustrations, cartoonish forms, bright multicolor sections, heavy shadows, and startup-SaaS visual clichés.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with one headline sentence: “Your [agent / runtime / environment / session], always ready. Only awake when it matters.” Rotate only the workload noun; keep the rest stable and at one display size.
2. Make technical truth feel elegant: measurements, lifecycle states, and capability boundaries are proof, not decoration.
3. Use restraint as confidence: neutral surfaces dominate, color is rare, and typography and spacing create hierarchy.
4. Preserve product honesty: distinguish manual and scheduled wake available today from request-driven wake on the roadmap.
5. Design accessibly by default: semantic HTML, keyboard-visible controls, readable line lengths and contrast, responsive composition, and a static reduced-motion alternative for changing hero terms.
